What “the model” actually does

Four jobs, shown on real maps

It is a pipeline of models and ordinary code—not one AI inventing a new map.

01FindA detector crops candidate symbols and legend rows.
02ReadOCR + a vision-language model return labels and boxes.
03TraceSAM2 proposes map regions; color matching assigns unit codes.
04ExportGeometry code writes polygons and places them on Earth.

Example 3 · Kechumstuk, Alaska

Image pixels get real coordinates

Tesseract reads border ticks. OpenCV locates them. A homography maps pixels to latitude and longitude.

What the numbers mean

Open the short methods note

Each number belongs only to its named frozen evaluation and denominator; results are not pooled across RCs. RC7/RC8 polygon sheets are public benchmark sheets, not blind. Field coverage measures presence or form, not factual correctness. Repeatability measures whether reads agree, not whether they are right. Picture overlays show actual pipeline artifacts; a box or mask alone is not an accuracy claim.
